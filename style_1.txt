Use a premium soft-pastel aesthetic with a dreamy, glow-infused atmosphere.
Visuals should combine elegance with subtle futuristic energy.

🎨 Color & Atmosphere

Soft gradient backgrounds or surfaces using purple, violet, indigo, and subtle orange highlights.

Semi-transparent layers (white/20–40) to create a frosted glass effect.

Use gentle color transitions; nothing harsh or flat.

🌫️ Effects & Texture

Frequent use of backdrop blur, translucency, and soft glows.

Add floating or glowing accents such as blurred blob-like shapes or ambient lighting.

Elements should feel airy, smooth, and slightly magical.

✨ Interactions & Motion

Transitions should be smooth (≈300ms), with soft easing—never abrupt.

Hover states should subtly scale elements and increase glow/shadow depth.

Selected/active states should use warm orange highlights, glow pulses, and slight scale-up.

Optional: subtle looping glow or ambient animation for premium emphasis.

🧪 Component Styling Rules

Corners should be fully rounded (2xl or more).

Use thin borders with soft color tones (purples/oranges) and light transparency.

Shadows should be soft, diffused, and used sparingly to emphasize interaction.

Typography should be clean, bold where needed, using slate/dark neutrals for contrast.

🌟 Overall Vibe

Dreamy, modern, soft-futuristic.

Warm highlights, cool base tones.

Blurred, glowing, premium aesthetic.

Everything should feel gentle, smooth, and visually comforting, but still high-end.

Important Note: 
Never use gradient bg or shadow for text, it hard to read.
